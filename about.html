THIS IS GAME DEV

ABOUT

An online community of game developers, artists, programmers, entrepreneurs and creatives coworking together.

This is a Game Dev is a community of digital creators who cowork online, keep each other company and support each other while working towards our creative passions.

The official Twitch stream is hosted by Lana Lux who works on her game STRAIN and hosts coworking sessions, segments and events live throughout the week.

People meet online to work together, discuss game development, science, tech, philosophy, etc.

While there are many regulars, anyone is welcome to join the community.

Conversations continue when the stream is offline on Discord. This is also where work can be shared.
