This is Game Dev

===================================

This is a community of digital creators who cowork online, keep each other company and support each other while working towards our creative passions.

The official live stream for the community is hosted on Twitch by Lana Lux.

People meet to work together, discuss game development, science, tech, philosophy, etc.

While there are many regulars, anyone is welcome to join the community.

Conversations continue when the stream is offline on Discord. This is also where work can be shared.







Stream segments

===================================



3D Modelling Challenge
  • A random object is picked from a wheel.
  • The challenge is to do a quick model of that object within 10 minutes.
  • Lana models hers on stream and chat is invited to join along in their 3D program of choice.
  • Everyone shares their art and Lana shows them on stream.
  • Other art forms are welcome.


Composition Challenge
  • A random theme is picked for the round.
  • Everyone gets one hours to make a scene in their game engine or 3D software.
  • Lana makes a composition over the hour.
  • At the end, everyone's results are shared.
  • It is encouraged to practice using the principles of art and design during the session.
  • Other art forms are welcome.


Digital Graffiti
  • By request, a chat member's username is picked.
  • Lana digitally illustrates it in a graffiti style.
  • The resulting graffiti tag is added to the game.


Pick an Object, Add it to the Game
  • Lana shares a list of objects she needs to add to the game.
  • Chat votes on one.
  • Lana 3D models the object, UV unwraps it, textures it and programs it into the game.
  • This could be an object that can be built, searched, something to craft with, plant, eat, etc.


Interactive Twitch Game
  • A 3D simulation game is run and shown on stream.
  • Anyone from chat can choose to join the 3d city sim, customize their character and join in on minigames.
  • Typing !join in the Twitch chat adds your username with a random avatar to the game.
  • !change to randomize your character (or !changebody !changeskin).
  • !fight during a fight to potentially be selected to battle.
  • !1-100 pick a number if you're selected to battle. The closest one to the random number wins an accessory.


Show and Tell
  • At the end of each stream, Lana goes through show and tell entries from Discord.
  • Images, video and audio clips of works in progress or recently finished mechanics, projects are all welcome, a good meal or your work station, etc.
  • As long as you made it or took the photo, etc. the community would love to see it and hear about it.
