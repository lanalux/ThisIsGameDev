STREAM FORMAT

SEGMENTS

Recurring formats, what to expect, and how to participate.

01

Creative Work Session

Focused co-working for game development, art, tech, writing and related creative work.

Rules

  • Share what you are working on, but keep feedback constructive.
  • No unsolicited promotion or repeated links.
02

3D Modelling Challenge

A timed or themed modelling exercise, from rough blockout to something unexpectedly good.

Rules

  • Follow the prompt and time limit announced on stream.
  • Original work only unless the challenge explicitly allows kitbashing.
  • Keep submissions stream-safe and credit third-party assets.
03

Interactive Twitch Game

Chat helps steer an on-stream game or creative experiment.

Rules

  • One vote / suggestion per round unless stated otherwise.
  • No griefing, harassment or attempts to derail the session.
  • The host has final say on what enters the game or project.
04

Composition Challenge

A live music / sound challenge built around a prompt, limitation or shared idea.

Rules

  • Use only material you have permission to use.
  • Keep suggestions concise so the session can keep moving.
  • Experimental is welcome. Volume wars are not.
05

Show & Tell

A place for members of the community to share work, progress, tools and strange little discoveries.

Rules

  • Submit only work you own or are authorized to present.
  • Label mature, flashing or potentially sensitive content.
  • Keep pitches short and useful to the audience.
06

Strain Dev + Q&A

Development sessions, problem solving and questions around the game and the process.

Rules

  • No spoilers or private project information unless it is shown on stream.
  • Questions are welcome, but avoid demanding release dates or private details.
  • Critique the work, not the person.
